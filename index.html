1. Overview

ECD Softwares ("we," "us," or "our") develops GalleryFlow, an Android app for browsing, viewing, editing, and managing photos, videos, animated GIFs, folders, and ZIP archives on a device, supported USB storage, and user-selected SMB network shares.

GalleryFlow is primarily designed to work with your media on your device or with storage locations that you choose. We do not operate a GalleryFlow cloud service that automatically receives or hosts your personal photos, videos, or ZIP archives. Accessing a network share, sharing a file, purchasing Premium, or contacting support can involve information being sent to the parties described below.

2. Information GalleryFlow accesses and processes

  • Media and file information: Photos, videos, GIFs, ZIP/archive contents, thumbnails, filenames, folder paths, file sizes, dates, and available metadata (including EXIF information, which may contain location coordinates embedded in a photo). The app uses this information to browse, preview, play, search, sort, edit, organize, copy, move, share, or delete the files you select.
  • Local storage and USB content: Files and folders made available through Android media permissions or storage access granted by you.
  • SMB/network connection details: When you configure network access, the app processes the server hostname or IP address, share name, port, username, domain, and any password you provide. It may discover accessible devices or shares on your connected network to support network browsing.
  • Local preferences and app state: Themes, layout settings, included/excluded folders, Favorites, administrator/lock settings, thumbnail caches, saved network profiles, and information needed to manage the 30-day Premium trial.
  • Purchase information: If you buy Premium, Google Play Billing handles the transaction and supplies the app with purchase status and transaction identifiers needed to unlock or restore your entitlement. GalleryFlow does not request or store your payment-card number.
  • Local diagnostics: The app stores limited local event and crash information to help diagnose performance and stability. The current diagnostic implementation avoids saving media contents, filenames, file paths, SMB addresses, usernames, passwords, and original exception messages in those logs. A report may include app and Android version details if you choose to export it.
  • Support communications: If you contact ECD Softwares, we may receive the contact details, message, attachments, or diagnostic report you voluntarily send.

3. How information is used

GalleryFlow uses the information described above to display and manage media, generate previews and caches, connect to network shares selected by you, save app preferences, protect restricted areas, manage trial and Premium access, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal information or use your private media for targeted advertising or AI-model training. The current GalleryFlow release does not include an advertising SDK or a third-party analytics SDK.

4. Network connections and file sharing

When you choose to connect to an SMB server, GalleryFlow communicates directly with that server over the network. Depending on the operation, this can involve transmitting connection credentials and requesting or transferring file contents between your device and the selected server. The security of SMB connections depends on the server, protocol configuration, and network; use trusted shares and networks.

When you explicitly share, export, or download media, the selected destination app, service, folder, or network server may receive the relevant file and its embedded metadata. Those destinations follow their own privacy and security practices. These transfers are initiated by your actions, not by an automatic upload of your gallery to ECD Softwares.

5. Storage, security, and retention

GalleryFlow stores settings, network profiles, trial state, caches, and diagnostics locally in app-controlled storage. Saved SMB passwords are encrypted with a key managed by the Android Keystore; saved server names, share addresses, and usernames are stored as part of the app's local connection profiles. Exported network-profile settings do not include saved passwords.

Generated thumbnails and temporary ZIP/network media caches may contain representations or copies of your files. You can use relevant cache-management controls when available. Removing a saved network profile removes its saved connection record and corresponding remembered password. Uninstalling GalleryFlow normally removes its app-private data, although files you saved outside app-private storage, original media, and files held by external services or SMB servers may remain.

Local settings and diagnostics are retained while needed for app functionality, until they are deleted, cleared, or removed with the app, subject to the storage behavior of Android. Information you send to support is kept only as reasonably necessary to address your request and meet applicable legal obligations. Google retains billing-related records under its own policies. No security system can guarantee absolute protection.

6. Permissions

Depending on Android version and features used, GalleryFlow may request access to photos/videos or selected media, older Android storage permissions, and access to files or USB locations that you choose through Android's file-access interface. Network and Wi-Fi permissions enable SMB connectivity and network discovery. Audio-setting access supports media playback behavior.

You can manage applicable permissions in Android Settings. Denying or revoking a permission may disable related browsing or file-management functionality. GalleryFlow does not request your device's live GPS location; however, media files you open may contain location metadata embedded by the device that created them.

7. Third-party services

  • Google Play / Google Play Billing: Processes optional Premium purchases, purchase restoration, and related transactions in accordance with Google's own terms and privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.
  • SMB servers and local-network devices: Servers and devices that you choose to connect to exchange the information needed for authentication and file access.
  • Apps and destinations you choose for sharing: Receive data only as part of an action you initiate using GalleryFlow's share, export, or similar functions.
  • Support providers: If you email us, your chosen email provider and our email provider process the communication according to their own practices.

We do not use an ECD Softwares-operated cloud gallery or account system in the current version of GalleryFlow.

8. Your choices and deletion requests

You can manage media permissions in Android Settings, remove saved SMB connections, clear supported caches, change privacy or administrator settings, delete app-managed information through available controls, and uninstall the app to remove most app-private data. Deleting or uninstalling the app does not automatically delete original media or external downloads stored elsewhere.

GalleryFlow currently does not require you to create a separate ECD Softwares account. If you contact us and want your support communications corrected or deleted, or want to exercise applicable privacy rights, use the privacy contact above. We will review requests under applicable law. Google Play purchase records and payment-related requests may also need to be handled through Google.

9. Children's privacy

GalleryFlow is a general-purpose media utility and is not specifically directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children for marketing. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us through a support request, please contact us so we can review it.

10. International processing

Support correspondence or optional purchase processing may involve service providers operating outside your country. Such processing is governed by the applicable provider's practices and relevant privacy laws.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect app changes, new services, or legal requirements. We will post an updated effective date here and, when appropriate, provide additional notice in the app or through the app listing.
